About Moe Dalz

Moe Dalz, also known as Abdallah Musa Makongoro, is a Tanzanian entrepreneur, musician and author. His interests bring together business, music, literature and creative expression.

Through his entrepreneurial journey, he aims to explore opportunities, build meaningful ventures and contribute to the growth of communities.

Tanfruits International

Moe Dalz is the founder of Tanfruits International, a Tanzanian agricultural business focused on connecting local fruit farmers with international markets.

The company's work focuses on fresh fruit, agricultural partnerships and opportunities for Tanzanian farming communities.
